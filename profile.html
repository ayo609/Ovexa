1. Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account on Ovexa, you agree to these terms. Please read them carefully before using our services.

2. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to open an Ovexa account and participate in any of our investment plans.

3. Account Responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials and for all activities under your account.

4. Wallet System

Ovexa operates on a two-wallet system:

  • Deposit Wallet — holds funds used to purchase investment plans
  • Withdrawal Wallet — holds earnings available for withdrawal

5. Investment Plans

All Ovexa plans run for 30 days and credit earnings to your withdrawal wallet at 12:00 AM daily, Monday through Saturday.

6. Withdrawals

Withdrawals are processed Monday to Saturday, 8:30 AM – 5:30 PM. A charge of 13% applies per withdrawal. Minimum withdrawal amount is ₦1,000.

7. Referral Program

Ovexa rewards users for referrals across three levels (10% / 3% / 1%) upon a downline's first investment purchase. Bonuses are credited to your withdrawal wallet.

8. Amendments

Ovexa may update these terms at any time. Continued use of our services implies acceptance of updated terms.

9. Contact

For questions about these terms, please reach out via our community channels.

1. Information We Collect

We collect your username, email address, phone number, and (for withdrawals) bank details. This information is required to operate your account securely.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process deposits, investments, and withdrawals
  • To send important account notifications
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent activity

3. Data Security

Your data is stored using Firebase (Google Cloud) with industry-standard encryption both in transit and at rest. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is only shared with trusted infrastructure providers (Firebase) strictly to operate the platform.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser session storage to keep you logged in. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

6. Your Rights

You may request to view, update, or delete your account data by contacting us through our community channels.

7. Updates to This Policy

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be posted here. Continued use of Ovexa implies acceptance of the updated policy.
